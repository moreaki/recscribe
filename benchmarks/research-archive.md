# Archived Whisper weight-space research

The rejected weight-space experiments are retained as research evidence outside
the two reusable infrastructure PRs, [#110](https://github.com/moreaki/recscribe/pull/110)
and [#111](https://github.com/moreaki/recscribe/pull/111). Closing an experiment PR
does not approve its candidates, merge its recipes, delete its branch or change
its scores. No experimental model or default is promoted.

## Reusable extraction

- #110 retains corpus/scoring and the bounded public/fresh acquisition helpers,
  including source/hash/group exclusions and original-byte preservation.
- #111 retains serial matrices, capability-validated opt-in research profiles,
  provenance, metrics and cancellation, plus the recording-priority worker guard.
  Single-repeat quality plans keep their separate repeat/stability requirement.
- Deterministic batching/permission-watcher test synchronization and the bounded
  signal-test cleanup are retained. Existing assertions and frozen fixtures are
  unchanged; main already contains the native CI diagnostic improvements.

The generic acquisition/guard and repeat/test fixes are extracted from the
retained round-3 head above. Weight-edit recipes, tensor-editing utilities,
activation probes, experiment-specific assessors/dependencies and multi-round
evidence archives remain on the frozen research refs, outside the infrastructure
PR diffs. Historical passes stay tied to their original source SHAs. Both current
infrastructure heads require their own full CI and review before any merge.
